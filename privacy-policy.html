Privacy Policy - Conflict Risk

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Conflict Risk ("the app") is built around places, not people. This policy explains what the app does with data. The short version: the app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not store your location.

Who is responsible Sergiy Kyrychenko, contact: sergiy.kyrychenko@moippo.mk.ua

What stays on your device Saved places, alerts, language, units and notification settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us and are not linked to your name, email or any account. Deleting the app deletes them.

What is sent to our server To show the risk for a place, the app sends the coordinates of that place (the place you searched for or saved, or your current location if you tap "Use my location") to our server over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and receives the result. Nothing else about you or your device is sent: no name, no email, no advertising identifier, no device identifier.

The server answers the request and does not keep it. We do not store coordinates, we keep no location history, and we do not build profiles. Our server software is configured not to write request logs.

Location The app asks for location access only when you tap "Use my location". The location is used once to look up the risk for that place. Location access can be switched off in the system settings at any time; you can search for places instead.

Notifications If you allow notifications, the app shows alerts on your device about the places you saved. The alert text is created on your device.

No analytics, advertising or tracking The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and shares no data with advertisers or data brokers.

Data sources The risk index is calculated by a fixed formula from public sources: ACLED (through the Humanitarian Data Exchange, OCHA), GDELT, GOV.UK travel advice, U.S. Department of State travel advisories, HDX displacement data (IOM DTM) and GDACS. These sources are public datasets about places and events. They do not contain personal data about app users. Conflict activity data is used under ACLED's Terms of Use and Attribution Policy; individual events are not shown. Map: OpenFreeMap, OpenMapTiles, OpenStreetMap contributors. Place names and borders: Natural Earth.

Children The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Your rights Because we do not store personal data, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. If you have a question, write to the address above.

Changes If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

Disclaimer Conflict Risk is an informational index calculated from third-party data. It is not an official security warning and not the statistical probability of war. Always follow the instructions of local authorities and official government travel advice.
