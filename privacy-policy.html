Privacy Policy - Conflict Risk

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Conflict Risk ("the app") is built around places, not people. This policy explains what the app does with data. The short version: the app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and our server does not store your location. Notifications are delivered by Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, which processes an installation ID (see Notifications below).

Who is responsible

Sergiy Kyrychenko, contact: sergiy.kyrychenko@moippo.mk.ua

What stays on your device

Saved places, alerts, language, units and notification settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us and are not linked to your name, email or any account. Deleting the app deletes them.

What is sent to our server

To show the risk for a place, the app sends the coordinates of that place (the place you searched for or saved, or your current location if you tap "Use my location") to our server over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and receives the result. Nothing else about you or your device is sent: no name, no email, no advertising identifier, no device identifier.

The server answers the request and does not keep it. We do not store coordinates, we keep no location history, and we do not build profiles. Our server software is configured not to write request logs.

Location

The app asks for location access only when you tap "Use my location". The location is used once to look up the risk for that place. Location access can be switched off in the system settings at any time; you can search for places instead.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app can tell you when the risk for a place you saved changes. To do this it subscribes your device to notification topics of Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: one topic per saved place's area (a grid square of about 25 x 25 km), per app language and per preview setting (full or private). Firebase (Google) keeps these subscriptions and delivers the messages. To deliver them, Google processes a Firebase installation ID (on iPhone also the Apple push token) together with technical data such as app version, device model, language, time zone and operating system version. Google handles this under its own terms and retention rules: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Our server only publishes a message to a topic. It never receives your installation ID, push token or the list of topics, and it cannot tell who is subscribed. Notifications you receive are also kept in the Alerts list on your device. Turning notifications off in the app (or in the system settings) removes the subscriptions, and uninstalling the app ends them.

No analytics, advertising or tracking

The app contains no analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase is not used), no advertising and no tracking, and shares no data with advertisers or data brokers.

Data sources

The risk index is calculated by a fixed formula from public sources: ACLED (through the Humanitarian Data Exchange, OCHA), GDELT, GOV.UK travel advice, U.S. Department of State travel advisories, HDX displacement data (IOM DTM) and GDACS. These sources are public datasets about places and events. They do not contain personal data about app users. Conflict activity data is used under ACLED's Terms of Use and Attribution Policy; individual events are not shown. Map: OpenFreeMap, OpenMapTiles, OpenStreetMap contributors. Place names and borders: Natural Earth.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Your rights

Because our server does not store personal data, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. The Firebase installation ID is held by Google under the rules linked above; we cannot see or delete it. If you have a question, write to the address above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

Disclaimer

Conflict Risk is an informational index calculated from third-party data. It is not an official security warning and not the statistical probability of war. Always follow the instructions of local authorities and official government travel advice.
