Common questions

Where does the risk come from? From a fixed formula applied to public sources (ACLED through HDX, GDELT, GOV.UK, U.S. State Department, GDACS). Open Settings > Data sources to see every source, its status and the date of its data.

Why does it say "Some data is missing or delayed"? One or more sources have not answered recently. The result is still shown, calculated from the sources that are available.

Is it a safety warning? No. It is an informational index. Follow local authorities and official government travel advice.

Does the app need an account or my location? No account. Location is used only if you tap "Use my location", only to look up the risk for that place, and is not stored.
